Two models, one conversation

Talk, type, show a photo, or go Live with the camera, and two of LiquidAI's models take turns on your device. d1-Omni-2B, the decision model, reads it first and decides what you want: your intent, how urgent it is, your mood, what is in view, whatever the profile asks. LFM3-Omni-2B then takes the same message and answers aloud, with that decision in mind. No API is called, and nothing leaves your device.

Both models load into the GPU at once: about 2.1 GB of 4-bit weights, and 0.4 GB more for pictures the first time you add a photo or open Live. A copy is kept in this browser's storage (shared with the chat and Decide pages, so anything they already saved is not downloaded again). Needs a GPU with room for both, Safari 26 or another current browser.
