d1-Omni-2B: decisions in the browser

LiquidAI's decision model runs on this device via WebGPU. Give it a message, some JSON, a photo or a recording, and ask it named questions: yes or no, one choice, several choices, or a score. It answers every question at once with calibrated probabilities, in one pass, without writing any text. Nothing leaves your device.

The weights (4-bit) stream into the GPU and a copy is kept in this browser's storage, so later visits skip the download. The image and speech parts load the first time you add a photo or a recording. Needs a GPU, Safari 26 or another current browser.
